# Research Charter

## Mission

Study whether complex observable behavior can arise from systems whose internal
subsystems occupy different activation, access, integration, or observation states.

The lab does not assume that one mechanism explains brains, AI agents, GPUs, or
memory systems. It tests whether a shared formal language is useful, then
requires domain-local evidence before any transfer claim.

## Research method

Each durable tranche follows:

1. prior-art / literature intake;
2. atomic decomposition;
3. source / observation / inference separation;
4. competing hypotheses;
5. pseudo-Council to convergence;
6. mathematical contract;
7. deterministic known-answer validation where possible;
8. experiment or public-data analysis;
9. failure injection / Red Team;
10. uncertainty and sensitivity analysis;
11. structured evidence;
12. claim + explicit limitations.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. deterministic known-answer validation;
2. direct neurophysiological or physical measurement from primary literature;
3. independent replication on public data;
4. controlled public-data model comparison;
5. review / synthesis;
6. synthetic sensitivity analysis;
7. cross-domain analogy.

A lower level cannot silently be promoted into a higher level.

## Public-repository rule

A canonical public claim must be reproducible from public sources, public data,
or deterministic generators committed here.

Private observations may inspire questions but cannot serve as the sole
evidence for a canonical public claim.

## Medical safety boundary

This repository does not conduct or encourage medication self-experiments.

No protocol may require zolpidem administration, medication rechallenge,
intentional induction of parasomnia, or other hazardous self-experimentation.

Somatosensory hypotheses do not authorize painful threshold-seeking, electrical
stimulation, invasive recording, injury, or unsupervised clinical-style
intervention. Those require separate safety, ethics, and domain-specific
protocols.

## Cross-domain transfer rule

~~~text
analogy
  ↓
formal correspondence candidate
  ↓
domain-local measurable prediction
  ↓
domain-local experiment
  ↓
independent evidence
  ↓
transferable claim candidate
~~~

A shared equation is not evidence of a shared physical mechanism.
