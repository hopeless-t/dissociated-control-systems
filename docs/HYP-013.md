# HYP-013 — Physics-Informed Skeleton-to-Envelope Reconstruction

> Status: OPEN HYPOTHESIS  
> Scope: synthetic control / drawing-model formalism  
> Biological interpretation: UNVALIDATED  
> Production authority: NONE

## Question

Can a self-improving controller reduce surface/anatomy error by using a kinematic
skeleton plus external loading/support constraints to infer a more plausible soft
envelope before final rendering?

## State

Let:

- q_i: joint positions,
- e=(i,j): bone edges,
- L_e: nominal bone lengths,
- m_k: region masses,
- g: gravity,
- c_j: support contacts,
- F_j: support reactions,
- Pi: projection/view mapping,
- r_e: soft-envelope parameters.

The drawing controller is not asked to solve full biomechanics. It only needs enough
structure to reject impossible or low-value surface hypotheses before paying the
full material/light/detail cost.

## Residual family

~~~text
R_len      bone-length consistency
R_joint    joint-range consistency
R_force    external-force balance
R_moment   projected moment balance
R_contact  support / penetration / stance-phase consistency
R_volume   soft-envelope conservation prior
R_proj     projection / foreshortening consistency
R_surface  joint continuity / stick-like envelope / self-intersection
~~~

Combined validator:

~~~text
E =
  w_len R_len
+ w_joint R_joint
+ w_force R_force
+ w_moment R_moment
+ w_contact R_contact
+ w_volume R_volume
+ w_proj R_proj
+ w_surface R_surface
~~~

## Gravity/support inversion

For a simple two-support projected static case:

~~~text
F_left + F_right = W

F_left * x_left + F_right * x_right = W * x_com
~~~

This gives a known-answer support split.

The support load can then be propagated as a *drawing prior*:

- higher loaded proximal regions receive more envelope thickness;
- distal hinge regions remain narrower;
- support paws can flatten more under larger reaction force;
- near/far projection scale is applied separately from load.

This is intentionally a control prior, not a muscle-activation model.

## Expected behavior

The controller should:

1. generate a cheap semantic skeleton;
2. solve/validate support and projection constraints;
3. derive a tapered soft-envelope hypothesis;
4. observe the surfaced result;
5. compare against the constant-width/tube baseline;
6. compile the relation only after held-out transfer.

## Falsifiers

The hypothesis is not supported for a regime if:

1. physics-informed envelope priors do not reduce held-out structural/surface error;
2. the added model cost exceeds the saved correction cost;
3. support/load priors repeatedly make stylized anatomy worse;
4. a simpler geometry-only baseline performs equally well or better.

## Claim ceiling

Synthetic success may establish only:

~~~text
PROJECTED_SUPPORT_SOLVER_IMPLEMENTED
LOAD_AWARE_ENVELOPE_PRIOR_IMPLEMENTED
KNOWN_ANSWER_STATIC_CHECK_VALIDATED
~~~

It cannot establish a veterinary biomechanics model, biological mechanism, or
general anatomical correctness.
