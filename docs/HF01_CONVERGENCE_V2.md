## 1. Current research question

HF01 no longer asks:

> Can awareness or behavior directly make hair regrow?

The current question is:

> Can a partially observed follicle system lose recoverability before visible
> output reveals that loss, and can a calibrated observer identify state,
> response susceptibility, structural context, and remaining reachability
> without laundering uncertainty into authority?

## 4. Biological state candidates

### R — regenerative-state candidate

Current support has three contexts:

1. GSE36169 paired human scalp transcript projection;
2. GSE93766 balding/non-balding DP-cell transcript projection;
3. Lu et al. 2016 human AGA protein/localization/perturbation evidence
   (PMID 27472703).

The first two share the frozen HF01 transcript-axis definition and therefore do
not provide fully independent projection families.

Adding the third orthogonal measurement family raises the synthetic evidence
root-cut for the broad R-state compatibility claim from 1 to 2.

Claim ceiling:

~~~text
cross-sectional / mechanism-state compatibility
!= validated R scalar
!= treatment-response meter
!= recoverability
~~~

### P — progenitor-reserve candidate

Garza et al. 2011 (PMID 21206086) reports preserved KRT15-high stem cells in
bald AGA scalp with marked depletion of CD200-high/ITGA6-high and CD34-high
progenitor populations.

Bazid et al. 2026 (PMID 41629248) reports preserved Sox9 staining with reduced
CD34 expression in frontal bald AGA tissue, including frontal-vs-occipital
within-person differences.

This supports separating:

~~~text
stem-cell presence
!= progenitor-state abundance / competence
~~~

It does not prove that CD34 uniquely measures P or that restoring a marker
restores hair.

### F* — structural-remodeling candidate

Cross-sectional transcript data retain an ECM/structural-remodeling signal.

But:

~~~text
F* structural remodeling
!= F synthetic mechanical lock
!= proven biological hysteresis
~~~

Human longitudinal path-dependence remains open.

## 5. Dynamic system-identification bridge

The 2026 human AGA organ model (PMID 42237333; PMCID PMC13274076) supplies a
source-bound architecture closer to HF01's desired dynamic experiment:

~~~text
known disturbance input:
    DHT

internal probes:
    beta-catenin, IGF-1, TGF-beta1, DKK1, p21, AR,
    versican, K15

functional outputs:
    follicle growth
    hair-cycle/catagen state
    matrix proliferation
    apoptosis

rescue inputs:
    minoxidil
    bicalutamide
~~~

Reported DHT effects include growth inhibition, earlier catagen, reduced
matrix proliferation, increased apoptosis, lower beta-catenin/IGF-1, and higher
TGF-beta1/DKK1/p21/AR; K15 remained unchanged. Minoxidil and bicalutamide
partially reversed growth inhibition.

This is a stronger input -> state -> function bridge than a cross-sectional
tissue comparison.

It is still an in-vitro/ex-vivo human organ model, not in-vivo longitudinal
human recoverability.

## 8. Assurance kernel V2

The current authority path is no longer just a four-item checklist.

A terminal empirical claim must satisfy, in one composed kernel:

~~~text
structured claim semantics
    ↓
claim-relative checkpoint protocol
    ↓
usable evidence witnesses
    ↓
explicit defeater disposition
    ↓
acyclic primitive-grounded support graph
    ↓
explicit trust roots
    ↓
version/freshness match
    ↓
claim authority
~~~

Additional constraints:

- authentic evidence must also be claim-relevant, scope-compatible, and
  reproducibly transformed;
- OPEN defeaters downgrade PASS to UNKNOWN;
- RESOLVED defeaters require a real usable resolution witness;
- support cycles do not count as independent justification;
- witness roots must actually be ancestors of the endpoint claim;
- stale dependency revisions invalidate an old PASS certificate;
- primitive evidence bottoms out in explicit trust assumptions rather than an
  infinite verification regress;
- structured claim semantics derive the strongest barrier class, so a
  reachability claim cannot self-label as descriptive.

## 9. Evidence topology

Checkpoint count and evidence diversity are separate quantities.

~~~text
Checkpoint Multiplicity != Evidence Diversity
~~~

For a role, HF01 measures a small exact root cut:

> the minimum number of declared source-root failures needed to destroy every
> usable witness for that role.

This yields a structural next-probe heuristic:

~~~text
probe value = increase in root cut / declared probe cost
~~~

A third dataset using the same frozen transcript axis can have zero root-cut
gain, while an orthogonal protein/localization assay can add one unit.

This is not a truth probability; it is dependency-topology value.

## 10. Defeater search

No recorded counterexample is not the same as an adequate counterexample search.

HF01 separates:

~~~text
No Defeaters Recorded
!=
No Defeaters Exist
~~~

A transparent synthetic stopping surrogate bounds the per-round new-defeater
rate after repeated zero-novelty adversarial rounds, while also requiring more
than one search-strategy family.

Meeting that surrogate permits pausing search.

It never proves completeness.
