# HYP-011 — Adaptive Representation Granularity

> Status: OPEN HYPOTHESIS
> Scope: synthetic control / meta-learning formalism
> Biological interpretation: UNVALIDATED
> Production authority: NONE

## Question

Can a self-improving controller learn not only actions and observation policy, but
also the **granularity of its own internal representation**?

The working hypothesis is that the best internal state is neither maximally detailed
nor minimally sized. It is the smallest representation that remains sufficient to
predict protected critic outcomes and support stable control.

## Representation ladder

A task may move through:

~~~text
coarse masses / silhouette
-> articulated joint chains
-> support/contact constraints
-> material/compliance
-> viewpoint/perspective
~~~

When a task family becomes predictable and transferable, the representation may be
compressed into a compiled skill.

When prediction residuals grow or transfer fails, it should decompile and expose
more latent structure again.

## Objective

For representation option g:

~~~text
J(g) =
    C_representation(g)
  + C_execution(g)
  + lambda * E[decision_error(g)]
~~~

Choose the lowest-cost g that satisfies declared hard-critic constraints.

This explicitly rejects:

~~~text
more internal structure == more objects
fewer objects == better compression
larger state == better world model
~~~

Semantic degrees of freedom are distinct from raw implementation size.

## Expansion triggers

Expand representation when one or more of the following occurs:

- prediction residual crosses threshold;
- the same failure repeats after local correction;
- a new pose/support/material regime is encountered;
- critic disagreement suggests latent-state aliasing;
- transfer from a compiled skill fails.

## Compression triggers

Compress representation when:

- held-out transfer repeatedly succeeds;
- removing a latent state does not harm protected-critic prediction;
- a stable action/observation trajectory can be compiled into a bounded skill;
- replay residuals remain low over the declared confidence region.

## Relation to HYP-009 / HYP-010

HYP-009 adapts the improvement loop.
HYP-010 compiles repeated successful trajectories.
HYP-011 controls the **state space in which those loops operate**.

Together:

~~~text
object loop        -> improve state/action
meta loop          -> improve observation/action policy
skill compiler     -> cache repeated successful trajectories
representation loop-> expand/decompile or compress/compile latent state
~~~

## Falsifiers

The hypothesis is not supported for a regime if:

1. a fixed representation performs equally well or better on held-out quality,
   transfer, and total cost;
2. semantic expansion does not reduce residuals or repeated failures;
3. compression does not reduce cost, or materially harms transfer;
4. raw implementation size predicts success as well as semantic-state structure.

## Claim ceiling

Synthetic success may establish only:

~~~text
REPRESENTATION_BUDGET_PRIMITIVES_DEFINED
RESIDUAL_TRIGGERED_EXPANSION_IMPLEMENTED
MINIMAL_SUFFICIENT_REPRESENTATION_SELECTION_VALIDATED
~~~

It cannot establish a biological learning mechanism or general intelligence claim.
